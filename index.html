Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for a position in your Human Resources team. I am currently Human Resources Executive at Amilla Maldives, where I joined in December 2023 as Human Resources Administrator and was promoted in October 2025. I would welcome the chance to bring my resort HR experience to your organisation.

In my current role I manage all expatriate visa and work permit matters, from applications and renewals to keeping accurate expiry records so that no document lapses unnoticed. I also coordinate staff arrivals, departures and transfers, and prepare new-hire arrival documents.

I supervise our HR Attendants team, and more recently I have taken on recruitment, conducting interviews and managing the onboarding of new hires. Working closely with staff and managers is the part of HR I enjoy most.
